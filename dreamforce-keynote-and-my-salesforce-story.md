# Dreamforce Keynote & My Salesforce Story

## My Salesforce Story

Salesforce is not a platform I learned only from a certification guide or from the PMO side of a project. My Salesforce story was built through years of enterprise transformation work—working with business leaders, product owners, architects, developers, integration teams, QA, data teams and end users to turn complicated customer and operational processes into working technology.

I have seen Salesforce evolve from a cloud CRM platform into a broader enterprise ecosystem spanning Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Field Service, Industries/Vlocity, Data Cloud, MuleSoft, Einstein and now Agentforce. That evolution mirrors my own work: from CRM and workflow transformation, through multi-cloud and integration programs, to AI-enabled service and autonomous-agent use cases.

My role has often been the bridge between strategy and engineering. I translate business outcomes into roadmaps, epics, user stories, architecture decisions, integrations, release plans, testing, adoption and measurable value. I understand what happens beneath the program plan: objects and fields, permissions, Flow, Apex, Lightning Web Components, OmniStudio, APIs, MuleSoft, data migration, CI/CD, SIT/UAT, cutover and production stabilization.

At Entergy/ComTec, I led Agile Salesforce multi-cloud delivery across Service Cloud, Experience/Community Cloud and Marketing Cloud in a large customer environment, including refinement of more than 2,500 user stories and coordination across development, integration, QA and business teams. At JLL, my experience included global Community/Experience Cloud delivery across AMER, EMEA and APAC. At Cencora/AmerisourceBergen, I led delivery involving Salesforce Voice, MuleSoft and Agentforce—bringing the story forward into AI-enabled customer service.

What has remained constant is the objective: Salesforce should not simply be installed; it should change how people work, improve the customer or employee experience, connect fragmented systems and produce measurable operating value.
