# Program Charter

## Vision
Deliver a scalable CRM platform that supports global consistency, regional business needs, and measurable operational outcomes.

## Workstreams
1. Business process & requirements
2. Platform architecture
3. Integrations & data
4. Security & access
5. Release management
6. Change, training & adoption
7. KPI / value realization

## Governance
- Executive Steering Committee: monthly
- Program Leadership: weekly
- Release Readiness: per release
- Risk / Dependency Review: weekly

## Success Measures
- Adoption
- SLA performance
- Release predictability
- Incident reduction
- OPEX reduction
- Business KPI improvement
